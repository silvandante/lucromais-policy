Privacy Policy

Lucro+ · Walker Dev · Effective: 16 September 2026

This policy explains how Walker Dev (“we”) handles personal data in the Lucro+ mobile app and related services (the “Application”).

Lucro+ is a pricing and cost calculator for sellers. It is not a social network. Your catalog is not public to other users.

1. Controller and contact

The controller is Walker Dev. Contact:

  • Email: annyufrr@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp support: +55 62 9634-7704

2. What we collect and why

  • Account: email, password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we do not see the password in clear text), and, if you use Google Sign-In, your Google name and profile photo.
  • Business data you enter: product names, costs, prices, card-machine fees, fixed costs, revenue, notes, and product photos. On the free plan this stays on your device. With an account and Pro, we also sync to our servers (Firebase) for backup and use on more than one device.
  • Technical and usage data: operating system, SDK identifiers, usage events (for example opening the app, saving a product, viewing the paywall), and crash reports, to keep the app working.
  • Subscription: purchase identifier and Pro entitlement status. Card numbers and store billing data are processed by Google Play or the App Store, not by Lucro+.
  • Support: if you email or message us on WhatsApp, we process that conversation to help you.

We do not sell personal data. The Application does not use third-party ads or advertising IDs.

3. Legal bases (LGPD)

We process data, as applicable, to perform the service contract, comply with law, pursue legitimate interests in security and service improvement that do not override your rights, and based on consent when required (for example camera or photo-library access).

4. Third parties

Processors that help run the Application include Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), Google Sign-In, Google Play / Apple App Store, NTP time checks, and WhatsApp if you start a support chat. See Firebase privacy.

We may also disclose data if required by law, to protect rights, investigate fraud, or respond to a competent authority.

5. International transfers

Google/Firebase typically process data on servers outside Brazil (including the United States). Creating an account or using Pro may involve that transfer, under the safeguards offered by those providers.

6. Retention

  • Cloud account and catalog: while the account exists and as needed to complete a deletion request.
  • On-device only data: until you uninstall or clear app data.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: generally up to 24 months, per Firebase settings.
  • Support messages: as needed to help you and, if required, to defend legal rights.

Uninstalling does not automatically delete a cloud account. Use section 8.

7. Your rights

Under Brazilian Law 13.709/2018 (LGPD) you may request confirmation, access, correction, anonymization, portability, information on sharing, withdrawal of consent, and deletion. Email annyufrr@gmail.com. You may also contact Brazil’s ANPD.

If you are a California resident, you may request to know, delete, and not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising under CCPA/CPRA.

8. Account and data deletion

To delete your account and associated server data (Firestore, Storage, Authentication), email annyufrr@gmail.com from the same address as your account with subject “Lucro+ account deletion”. We will delete or anonymize within 30 days, except where law requires retention.

Store purchases remain under Google or Apple rules. Cancel subscriptions in the store settings; deleting the account alone does not cancel billing.

9. Children

Lucro+ is intended for business pricing use, not for children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. A parent or guardian may contact us to delete such an account.

10. Security and incidents

We use authentication, per-user access rules, and encrypted transport. No system is perfectly secure. We will notify you of relevant incidents as required by LGPD.

11. Changes

We may update this policy on this page with a new effective date. Prior versions are available on request.

12. Consent

By creating an account, uploading a photo, or granting system permissions, you agree to the processing described here. You can revoke camera or gallery permission in device settings; that stops new photos but does not delete existing ones until you remove them or request account deletion.
